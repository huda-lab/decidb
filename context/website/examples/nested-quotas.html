Hire under nested quotas

At most one hire per team, two per division and four overall, maximizing total score.

Query rewritingILP
decidb · nested-quotas.sql
SELECT name, division, team, score, hire
FROM Candidates
DECIDE hire(BOOL)
SUCH THAT SUM(hire) by (division, team) <= 1
      AND SUM(hire) by (division) <= 2
      AND SUM(hire) <= 4
MAXIMIZE SUM(score * hire);

The problem

The quotas nest: every team lies inside one division, and every division inside the company. Quotas that nest like this still have a greedy exact answer, so DeciDB rewrites the query and calls no solver.

Answered by query rewriting: DeciDB computes the optimum with sorting and joins, and no solver is called. EXPLAIN names the rule, S2_LAMINAR_MATROID.

How it reads

SUM(hire) by (division, team) <= 1
Without a per, a grouped sum bounds each row's own group: every team gets the limit.
SUM(hire) by (division) <= 2
A coarser group containing the first.
SUM(hire) <= 4
The whole company.

The answer

Ed, Ada, Jo and Hu. Fa has the second-highest score but shares the Data team with Ed. Cy is the best Mobile candidate, but Product already has its two hires, Ada and Jo, who score higher.
