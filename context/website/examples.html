First problems

One decision per row, a budget or a target, and an objective. Start here.

  • Pack the most valuable bagChoose which items to carry so their total weight stays under a limit and their total value is as high as possible.DECIDE x(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(x * weight) <= 50 MAXIMIZE SUM(x * value)ILP
  • Meet demand at the lowest costDecide how many units each plant makes, within its capacity, so that total output meets demand at the lowest cost.DECIDE units(INT) SUCH THAT units <= capacity AND SUM(units) >= 500 MINIMIZE SUM(units * unit_cost)ILP
  • A cheap diet that meets nutrition targetsPick servings of each food so protein, calories and fat land within their targets at the lowest cost.DECIDE servings(INT) SUCH THAT SUM(servings * protein) >= 90 AND SUM(servings * calories) BETWEEN 1800 AND 2200ILP
  • Category rules and a budget from a tableRules that apply to one category at a time, and limits read from a settings table instead of written into the query.SUCH THAT SUM(pick * cost) <= (SELECT budget FROM Settings) AND when category = 'A': SUM(pick) >= 2 AND when category = 'B': SUM(pick) <= 1ILP

Groups and keys

per, when and by write one constraint per group; a keyed declaration shares one decision across the rows of a key.

  • Cover every shiftExactly one nurse per shift, at most one shift per nurse. There is nothing to optimize, only rules to satisfy.SUCH THAT per shift: SUM(on_duty) by (shift) = 1 AND per nurse: SUM(on_duty) by (nurse) <= 1Feasibility
  • Assign people to tasksEvery task gets exactly one person, nobody gets two tasks, and the total fit score is as high as possible.SUCH THAT per worker: SUM(assign) by (worker) <= 1 AND per task: SUM(assign) by (task) = 1 MAXIMIZE SUM(assign * score)ILP
  • A diversified portfolioPick five stocks with the highest expected return, within a risk budget and at most two per sector.SUCH THAT SUM(pick) = 5 AND SUM(pick * risk) <= 30 AND per sector: SUM(pick) by (sector) <= 2ILP
  • One decision per nurse, not per shiftAfter a join each nurse appears once per shift, but the decision is about the nurse. A keyed declaration makes one decision per nurse.DECIDE per n: keep(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(keep * s.hours) <= 60 MAXIMIZE SUM(per n: keep * n.skill_score)ILP
  • Which depots to openOpen depots and route shipments so every customer's demand is met, paying a fixed cost for each open depot plus a cost per unit shipped.DECIDE per D: open(BOOL), ship(INT) SUCH THAT per C: SUM(ship) by (C) >= C.demand AND per D: SUM(ship) by (D) <= D.capacity * open MINIMIZE SUM(R.unit_cost * ship) + SUM(D: D.opening_cost * open)MILP

Beyond a sum

Make the worst case as good as possible, change as little as possible, or stay close to a target.

  • Share a shortage fairly across regionsSupply is short. Ship so that no region falls short of its demand by more than necessary. This is the DeciDB paper's Example 1.DECIDE ship(INT), per (): max_shortfall(INT) SUCH THAT per g: g.demand - SUM(ship) by (g) <= max_shortfall MINIMIZE max_shortfallILP
  • Balance work across machinesAssign each job to one of two machines so the busier machine finishes as early as possible.SUCH THAT per J: SUM(run) by (J) = 1 MINIMIZE max(per M.machine: sum(run * J.hours) by (M.machine))ILP
  • Raise revenue with as few price changes as possibleReach a revenue target by changing prices, keeping the largest change small and touching at most two items.SUCH THAT SUM(new_price * weekly_qty) >= 16500 AND norm(new_price - price, 0) <= 2 MINIMIZE norm(new_price - price, 'inf')MILP
  • Rebalance toward target weightsThe targets add up to more than 100% and one asset has a cap. Find the allowed weights closest to the targets.DECIDE w(REAL) SUCH THAT w <= max_weight AND SUM(w) = 1 MINIMIZE SUM(POWER(w - target, 2))QP

Answered by query rewriting

These problems have an exact answer that DuckDB computes with sorting, ranking and aggregation. DeciDB recognizes them and rewrites the query to compute it, with no solver and no change to the SQL you write.

  • A shortlist: the best two per genrePick at most two films per genre with the highest total rating. DeciDB ranks rows instead of calling a solver.DECIDE pick(BOOL) SUCH THAT per genre: SUM(pick) by (genre) <= 2 MAXIMIZE SUM(rating * pick)Query rewriting
  • Hire under nested quotasAt most one hire per team, two per division and four overall, maximizing total score.SUCH THAT SUM(hire) by (division, team) <= 1 AND SUM(hire) by (division) <= 2 AND SUM(hire) <= 4Query rewriting
  • Fit as many tickets as possible into a sprintWith 20 story points of capacity, take the largest number of tickets.DECIDE take(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(points * take) <= 20 MAXIMIZE SUM(take)Query rewriting
  • Allocate a budget fairlySplit 100 units among programs so that the worst-served program gets as large a share of its need as possible.DECIDE alloc(REAL) SUCH THAT alloc <= max_alloc AND SUM(alloc) <= 100 MAXIMIZE MIN(alloc / need)Query rewriting
  • Match drivers to pickupsPair four drivers with four pickups along a road so the total distance driven is as small as possible.SUCH THAT per d: SUM(x) by (d) = 1 AND per p: SUM(x) by (p) = 1 MINIMIZE SUM(ABS(d.km - p.km) * x)Query rewriting
  • Place one depot per highwayChoose a mile marker for one depot on each highway that minimizes the total distance to that highway's stores.DECIDE per highway: depot(REAL) SUCH THAT depot <= 100 MINIMIZE SUM(ABS(depot - mile))Query rewriting

When there is no answer

A query whose constraints cannot all hold tells you which clause to change.

  • Find out why there is no answerFour items in a 40 kg bag is impossible. DIAGNOSE names the clause to change and by how much.DIAGNOSE SELECT item, x FROM Items DECIDE x(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(x * weight) <= 40 AND SUM(x) >= 4ILP
