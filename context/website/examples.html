Examples

20 complete problems, each with its data, its query and the answer DeciDB returns. Every query on these pages is run on DeciDB when the site is built.

First problems

One decision per row, a budget or a target, and an objective. Start here.

Pack the most valuable bag

Choose which items to carry so their total weight stays under a limit and their total value is as high as possible.

DECIDE x(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(x * weight) <= 50 MAXIMIZE SUM(x * value)
ILPBOOL

Meet demand at the lowest cost

Decide how many units each plant makes, within its capacity, so that total output meets demand at the lowest cost.

DECIDE units(INT) SUCH THAT units <= capacity AND SUM(units) >= 500 MINIMIZE SUM(units * unit_cost)
ILPINT

A cheap diet that meets nutrition targets

Pick servings of each food so protein, calories and fat land within their targets at the lowest cost.

DECIDE servings(INT) SUCH THAT SUM(servings * protein) >= 90 AND SUM(servings * calories) BETWEEN 1800 AND 2200
ILPINTBETWEEN

Category rules and a budget from a table

Rules that apply to one category at a time, and limits read from a settings table instead of written into the query.

SUCH THAT SUM(pick * cost) <= (SELECT budget FROM Settings) AND when category = 'A': SUM(pick) >= 2 AND when category = 'B': SUM(pick) <= 1
ILPwhensubquery

Groups and keys

per, when and by write one constraint per group; a keyed declaration shares one decision across the rows of a key.

Cover every shift

Exactly one nurse per shift, at most one shift per nurse. There is nothing to optimize, only rules to satisfy.

SUCH THAT per shift: SUM(on_duty) by (shift) = 1 AND per nurse: SUM(on_duty) by (nurse) <= 1
Feasibilityperbyno objective

Assign people to tasks

Every task gets exactly one person, nobody gets two tasks, and the total fit score is as high as possible.

SUCH THAT per worker: SUM(assign) by (worker) <= 1 AND per task: SUM(assign) by (task) = 1 MAXIMIZE SUM(assign * score)
ILPperbysubquery

A diversified portfolio

Pick five stocks with the highest expected return, within a risk budget and at most two per sector.

SUCH THAT SUM(pick) = 5 AND SUM(pick * risk) <= 30 AND per sector: SUM(pick) by (sector) <= 2
ILPperby

One decision per nurse, not per shift

After a join each nurse appears once per shift, but the decision is about the nurse. A keyed declaration makes one decision per nurse.

DECIDE per n: keep(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(keep * s.hours) <= 60 MAXIMIZE SUM(per n: keep * n.skill_score)
ILPper K: x(TYPE)SUM(per K: …)

Which depots to open

Open depots and route shipments so every customer's demand is met, paying a fixed cost for each open depot plus a cost per unit shipped.

DECIDE per D: open(BOOL), ship(INT) SUCH THAT per C: SUM(ship) by (C) >= C.demand AND per D: SUM(ship) by (D) <= D.capacity * open MINIMIZE SUM(R.unit_cost * ship) + SUM(D: D.opening_cost * open)
MILPper D: open(BOOL)colon form

Beyond a sum

Make the worst case as good as possible, change as little as possible, or stay close to a target.

Share a shortage fairly across regions

Supply is short. Ship so that no region falls short of its demand by more than necessary. This is the DeciDB paper's Example 1.

DECIDE ship(INT), per (): max_shortfall(INT) SUCH THAT per g: g.demand - SUM(ship) by (g) <= max_shortfall MINIMIZE max_shortfall
ILPper (): x(TYPE)min-max

Balance work across machines

Assign each job to one of two machines so the busier machine finishes as early as possible.

SUCH THAT per J: SUM(run) by (J) = 1 MINIMIZE max(per M.machine: sum(run * J.hours) by (M.machine))
ILPmax(per k: sum(…) by (k))

Raise revenue with as few price changes as possible

Reach a revenue target by changing prices, keeping the largest change small and touching at most two items.

SUCH THAT SUM(new_price * weekly_qty) >= 16500 AND norm(new_price - price, 0) <= 2 MINIMIZE norm(new_price - price, 'inf')
MILPnorm(e, 'inf')norm(e, 0)

Rebalance toward target weights

The targets add up to more than 100% and one asset has a cap. Find the allowed weights closest to the targets.

DECIDE w(REAL) SUCH THAT w <= max_weight AND SUM(w) = 1 MINIMIZE SUM(POWER(w - target, 2))
QPPOWER(e, 2)convex QP

Answered without a solver

These problems have an exact answer that DuckDB computes with sorting, ranking and aggregation. DeciDB recognizes them and skips the solver, with no change to the query.

A shortlist: the best two per genre

Pick at most two films per genre with the highest total rating. DeciDB ranks rows instead of calling a solver.

DECIDE pick(BOOL) SUCH THAT per genre: SUM(pick) by (genre) <= 2 MAXIMIZE SUM(rating * pick)
Direct solveILPS1

Hire under nested quotas

At most one hire per team, two per division and four overall, maximizing total score.

SUCH THAT SUM(hire) by (division, team) <= 1 AND SUM(hire) by (division) <= 2 AND SUM(hire) <= 4
Direct solveILPS2by (a, b)

Fit as many tickets as possible into a sprint

With 20 story points of capacity, take the largest number of tickets.

DECIDE take(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(points * take) <= 20 MAXIMIZE SUM(take)
Direct solveILPS3

Allocate a budget fairly

Split 100 units among programs so that the worst-served program gets as large a share of its need as possible.

DECIDE alloc(REAL) SUCH THAT alloc <= max_alloc AND SUM(alloc) <= 100 MAXIMIZE MIN(alloc / need)
Direct solveLPR4MAXIMIZE MIN(…)

Match drivers to pickups

Pair four drivers with four pickups along a road so the total distance driven is as small as possible.

SUCH THAT per d: SUM(x) by (d) = 1 AND per p: SUM(x) by (p) = 1 MINIMIZE SUM(ABS(d.km - p.km) * x)
Direct solveILPO1CROSS JOIN

Place one depot per highway

Choose a mile marker for one depot on each highway that minimizes the total distance to that highway's stores.

DECIDE per highway: depot(REAL) SUCH THAT depot <= 100 MINIMIZE SUM(ABS(depot - mile))
Direct solveLPA2per K: x(REAL)

When there is no answer

A query whose constraints cannot all hold tells you which clause to change.

Find out why there is no answer

Four items in a 40 kg bag is impossible. DIAGNOSE names the clause to change and by how much.

DIAGNOSE SELECT item, x FROM Items DECIDE x(BOOL) SUCH THAT SUM(x * weight) <= 40 AND SUM(x) >= 4
ILPDIAGNOSE
