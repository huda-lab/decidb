# PER Keyword — Planned Features

`PER` is planned to move in front of the constraint and to stop grouping aggregates by
itself; a postfix `by (...)` on each aggregate takes over that job. The plan, its
decisions and its tests are in
[`../../09_anr_language/todo.md`](../../09_anr_language/todo.md).

Nothing else is open here. The trailing inner-aggregate binding rule is settled for the
supported two-level form and recorded in `done.md`.
