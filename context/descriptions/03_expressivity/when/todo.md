# WHEN Keyword — Planned Features

`WHEN` is planned to move in front of the clause it filters (`when c: ...`) and inside
the aggregate it filters (`sum(when c: ...)`), replacing both postfix forms. The plan,
its decisions and its tests are in
[`../../09_anr_language/todo.md`](../../09_anr_language/todo.md).

No other open gaps.
